How the System Works
This web application manages a team hierarchy where users are created by higher roles, track attendance, submit reports, monitor performance, and add clients, all while maintaining strict data privacy and role-based access. Here’s a breakdown:

User Roles and Hierarchy
Admin: Created by the system, manages Managers.
Managers: Created by Admin, manage Sales Staff.
Sales Staff: Created by Managers, manage Agents.
Agents: Created by Sales Staff, divided into:
Team Leaders: Lead groups of Sales Agents.
Individual Agents: Work solo, report directly to Sales Staff.
Sales Agents: Part of a group, report to their Team Leader.
Key Functionalities by Role
Admin:
Create, delete, update Managers.
View all system activity logs.
Reset user passwords (removes password, allowing login without it).
Receive and resolve login help requests from users.
Managers:
Create, delete, update Sales Staff.
View performance of all Agents created by their Sales Staff.
Receive reports submitted by Sales Staff.
Check attendance of Agents under their Sales Staff.
Sales Staff:
Create, delete, update Agents (Team Leaders, Individual Agents, Sales Agents).
Assign Sales Agents to existing groups or Team Leaders to lead groups (existing or new).
Set attendance time frames for their Agents.
Excuse late Agents, allowing them to add clients.
View performance and reports of all their Agents (Individual Agents directly, Sales Agents via Team Leaders).
Submit collected reports to their Manager.
Team Leaders (a type of Agent):
Lead a group of Sales Agents.
Add clients (if attended or excused).
Collect reports from their Sales Agents, add their own, and submit to Sales Staff.
View performance of their Sales Agents and themselves.
Check attendance of their group members.
Individual Agents:
Add clients (if attended or excused).
Submit reports directly to their Sales Staff.
View their own performance.
Sales Agents (grouped under Team Leaders):
Add clients (if attended or excused).
Submit reports to their Team Leader.
View their own performance.
Core Workflows
Attendance:
Agents must attend daily within a time frame set by their Sales Staff.
Attendance includes insurance sector and location.
If late, Sales Staff can excuse them, allowing client additions; excused status is recorded.
Unattended or unexcused Agents cannot add clients.
Reporting:
Sales Agents → Team Leader → Sales Staff → Manager.
Individual Agents → Sales Staff → Manager.
Reports include comments and are submitted periodically (weekly, monthly, quarterly).
Performance:
Viewed periodically (weekly, monthly, quarterly).
Access is hierarchical: Managers see all Agents via Sales Staff, Sales Staff see their Agents, etc.
Authentication:
Users are created with a work ID and email by a higher role.
Initial login uses work ID and email; passwords are optional and set later.
Forgot password triggers Admin reset (removes password); login help requests go to Admin.
Admin has no password.
Data Privacy and Integrity:
No user sees activities outside their hierarchy (e.g., Managers can’t see other Managers’ data).
Deleting a user doesn’t affect their data or managed users.
Additional Notes:
Work IDs are unique and unchangeable.
Admin can notify all users.
Sales Staff set independent time frames for their Agents.
